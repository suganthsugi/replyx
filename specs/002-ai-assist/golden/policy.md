# Acme Tags: returns, refunds and shipping

Acme Tags sells printed and plain hang tags to clothing and gift businesses in India. Prices are in Indian rupees (₹).

## Returns

- Plain (non-custom) tags can be returned within **30 days of delivery**, unused and in their original packaging.
- **Custom-printed tags cannot be returned**, because they are made to order. The only exception is a defective or wrong print (see "Damaged or wrong items").
- The customer pays the return shipping, unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.
- To start a return, reply in this chat with the order number. Our team sends a return label and the address.

## Refunds

- A refund goes back to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the returned items.
- An order canceled **before it is dispatched** gets a full refund within **3–5 business days**.
- Shipping fees are not refunded, unless the return is because of our mistake.
- We do not give discounts, coupons or price matches through support.

## Shipping

- We ship **within India only**. We do not ship internationally.
- Plain tags are dispatched within **2 business days**. Custom-printed orders are dispatched within **5–7 business days**, after the proof is approved.
- Standard delivery takes **3–6 business days** after dispatch.
- Standard shipping costs **₹99**, and is **free for orders over ₹2,000**.
- **Express delivery** (1–2 business days after dispatch) costs **₹249**.
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships.

## Damaged or wrong items

- Report damaged, defective or wrongly printed items within **7 days of delivery**, with photos.
- We then send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.

## Order changes

- The delivery address can be changed only **before the order is dispatched**. Our team makes the change; ask in this chat.
- After dispatch, the address cannot be changed.
