---
title: Chatting with support
description: How customers talk to support in one continuous chat, send files, and manage their profile.
---

Once you're signed in (see [Signing in](/guides/signing-in/)), the support
page is a single chat with the team. Everything you've ever asked is in the
same conversation, oldest at the top.

## Sending a message

Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** to send. Press
**Shift+Enter** to start a new line without sending.

Under each of your messages you'll see how far it has got:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Sending…** | Still on its way. |
| **Sent ✓** | We have it. |
| **Delivered ✓✓** | Someone on the team has opened your conversation. |
| **Read ✓✓** | The team has read it and replied. |

If a message can't be sent (for example, you lost your connection), it's
marked **Not sent** with a **Try again** button. Trying again never creates a
duplicate: the team sees the message once.

### Sending a lot at once

You can send up to **20 messages a minute**. If you go faster, the chat asks
you to wait and counts down the seconds until you can send again. Anything you
were typing stays in the box.

## Replies

Replies from the team appear on the left with the name of the person who
wrote them. While someone is writing to you, you'll see **"{name} is
typing"** with three moving dots. A line under the conversation tells you
where things stand, for example **Support has your message** or **Support has
replied**.

The chat stays up to date on its own. If you have it open in more than one tab
or device, all of them show the same conversation.

If your connection drops, a **Reconnecting…** banner appears. Once you're back
online, anything you missed shows up automatically.

## Past conversations

When an issue is wrapped up, a dated divider appears in the chat: **"Glad we
could help, just reply if you need anything else"**. If you have something
new or more to add, just write in the same chat. You never have to start over
or find an old thread.

Scroll to the top (or choose **Show earlier messages**) to load older
messages.

## Attaching files

Choose **Attach a file** (the paperclip) next to the message box. You can
attach up to **10 files** to one message, each up to **25 MB**.

Allowed files are images, PDFs, office documents, text files and zip
archives. Programs and scripts can't be sent. If a file isn't allowed or is
too large, the chat says so straight away and nothing is uploaded.

Every file is checked for malware before anyone can open it. Until the check
is done the file shows **checking the file…**. If a file looks unsafe it's
blocked and can't be downloaded.

## If you're away when support replies

If you're not in the chat when the team replies, you'll get an email with a
link back to the conversation (at most one every couple of minutes). The
email doesn't include the reply itself. You can turn these emails off in your
profile.

## Your profile

Open your profile from the **⋯** button at the top of the chat. There you can:

- change your **name** and **photo**,
- turn **Email me when support replies and I'm not here** on or off,
- set a **password** (optional: you can always sign in with an emailed link
  instead), or change it if you already have one,
- **Sign out** on this device, or **Sign out everywhere** to end your session
  on every device.
