---
title: Notification preferences
description: Choose which events notify you in the app, as desktop notifications and by email, pause alerts with the master switch, and set your organization's defaults.
---

ReplyX can tell you about the same event in three places: the notification center in the app, a
desktop notification from your browser, and an email. You choose which events use which of them.
For the list of events, see [Views and notifications](/guides/views-and-notifications/).

## Open your preferences

Open your profile and select **Notification preferences**, or select **Preferences** at the top of
the **Notifications** page. The page shows one row per event and a column for each channel:
**In-app**, **Desktop** and **Email**. Tick the boxes you want and select **Save preferences**.

Your organization sets the starting choices. Once you save, the whole grid is yours: later changes
to the organization's defaults don't change it.

## Turn on desktop notifications

Desktop notifications need your browser's permission, once per browser.

1. On the preferences page, select **Turn on desktop notifications**.
2. When your browser asks, choose **Allow**.
3. Make sure the **Desktop** box is ticked for the events you want.

Clicking a desktop notification opens the ticket in ReplyX. If your browser says notifications are
blocked for this site, allow them in the browser's site settings and select the button again. Use
**Re-register this browser** if notifications stop arriving after you cleared your browser data.
Each browser you use needs its own permission.

## Email notifications

Ticked **Email** boxes send an email to your sign-in address, with a link to the ticket. Email is
off for every event until you or your organization turn it on.

## Bursts become one alert

When a customer sends several messages on the same ticket, you get one desktop notification and one
email for the burst, sent **2 minutes** after the first message and saying how many arrived. The
same event never alerts you twice on the same channel.

## Pause everything with the master switch

Turn off **Desktop and email notifications** to stop every desktop notification and email at once,
for example on holiday. Your notification center keeps recording everything while the switch is
off, so nothing is lost; it just doesn't alert you. Turn the switch back on and save to resume.

A burst that was waiting to be sent when you turned the switch off isn't sent.

## For admins: set your organization's defaults

If you can edit organization settings, open **Admin** and then **Notification defaults**. The same
grid sets the channels each event uses for people who haven't saved their own preferences. Select
**Save defaults** to apply your changes. People who already saved their own preferences keep them.
Each change is recorded in the [audit log](/admin/audit-and-retention/).
