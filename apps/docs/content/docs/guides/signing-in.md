---
title: Signing in
description: How staff and customers sign in, reset passwords, and stay signed in.
---

Staff and customers sign in differently. Staff always use a password.
Customers usually sign in with an emailed link, and can optionally add a
password.

## Staff sign-in

Go to `/desk/sign-in` and enter your email and password.

### Locked out

After **5 failed attempts**, your account is locked for **15 minutes**. Wait
and try again, or use **Forgot password**.

### Forgot password

Select **Forgot password** and enter your email. If the email matches an
account, you'll get a reset link. The link is valid for **30 minutes** and
can be used **once**. Setting a new password ends all your other sessions.

The response to a password-reset request is the same whether or not the
email has an account, so no one can use it to find out who has an account.

### Sign out everywhere

From your profile, choose **Sign out of all sessions** to end every session
on every device, including any open real-time connections.

### Session expiry

Staff sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity.

## Customer sign-in

Customers sign in from the chat widget or support page with just their
email — no password required by default.

### Sign-in link

1. Enter your email. If self-registration is allowed and no account exists
   yet, you can also give your name to create one.
2. Check your email for a sign-in link.
3. Open the link to sign in.

The link:

- Is valid for **15 minutes**.
- Can be used **once**.
- Is replaced by any newer link you request — only the latest link works.

You can request at most **5 links per hour** for the same email address. The
response is the same whether or not the email has an account, so no one can
use it to find out who has an account.

### Keep me signed in

On a trusted device, choose **keep me signed in** to stay signed in for up
to **30 days** without requesting a new link each time.

### Optional password

You can set a password from your profile. Once set, you can sign in with
either the emailed link or your email and password.

### Sign out everywhere

Choose **Sign out of all sessions** to end your session on every device.
