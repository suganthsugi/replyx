---
title: Customer profile and satisfaction
description: Open a customer's full profile to see their details, tickets and whole conversation history, and read the satisfaction ratings customers leave after you resolve their issue.
---

The customer profile brings everything about one customer onto one page. Satisfaction ratings show
how customers felt about the help they got.

## Open a customer's profile

Select the customer's name on a ticket, or on a row in the inbox, to open the customer panel. It
shows their contact details, tags, and open and closed tickets. Select **Open full profile** for
the whole picture:

- **Contact details**: phone, company, when they became a customer and when they last wrote.
- **Tags** on the customer.
- **Satisfaction**: the average of their ratings and how many they gave.
- **Open tickets** and **Closed tickets**. Select one to open it.
- **Conversation history**: every message on their tickets, newest first, each with its ticket
  number. Internal notes are marked **Internal note**. Select **Load older messages** to go
  further back.

You only see tickets in groups you can view. Tickets in other groups add nothing to the lists, the
history or the satisfaction figure, so two people can see different profiles of the same customer.
See [Roles and groups](/admin/roles-and-groups/).

To change contact details or tags, use **Edit** in the customer panel; that needs permission to
edit users.

## Satisfaction ratings

When you resolve a customer's issue, their chat offers a quick question: **How did we do?** They
pick a score from **1** (very poor) to **5** (great) and can add a comment, or select **Skip**. It's
optional, and a skipped rating isn't asked again for that issue.

- The rating appears on the ticket as **Customer rating: 4/5**, with the comment when there is
  one.
- The customer can change their rating later; the ticket keeps one rating, the latest.
- The offer is shown while the issue is resolved. Once the customer writes again, the chat moves on.

Customers only ever see "How did we do?". They never see ticket numbers, groups or states.

## Satisfaction on the dashboard

The dashboard's **Customer satisfaction** figure is the average rating given in the last **7 days**,
with the number of ratings. Like every dashboard figure, it counts only tickets you can view.
Skipped ratings don't count. See [SLA and business hours](/admin/sla-and-business-hours/) for the
other dashboard figures.
